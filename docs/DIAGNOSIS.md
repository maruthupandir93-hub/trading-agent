## Part 3 — Issues found in this pass
### I-1 · CRITICAL — Per-trade risk is 13–20% of the account, and the approval text says otherwise
`agents/cro_agent.py:431-433`:
 allocation_governs = honour_session_allocation() and session_is_driving() if implied_var > var_limit and not allocation_governs: ...reject
`HONOUR_SESSION_ALLOCATION` defaults to **ON**, at the operator's own request("$5 with 10x leverage ... $50 trading value for each trade"). With it on, theVaR veto is **skipped** for every session trade, and the Risk Gateway stopssizing down. The result in the live approvals:
| Trade | Cost at stop | VaR limit | Share of equity ||---|---|---|---|| DOGE | $0.55 | $0.49 | 13.4% || LTC | $0.70 | $0.46 | **18.0%** || XRP Grid | $0.51 | $0.43 | 14.1% || SOL (open) | $0.65 | $0.39 | **20.3%** |
At a 33–40% win rate, losing streaks of 4–5 are normal. At 15–20% per loss, astreak of that length removes 50–65% of the account.
**Second bug in the same lines:** the approval text still says`implied VaR $0.65 ... within $0.39`. The check was skipped, and the recordclaims it passed. The text should say "VaR check skipped: session allocationgoverns (cost 20.3% of equity)".
**Fix (settings, no code):** either set `HONOUR_SESSION_ALLOCATION=false`, orlower the risk. Use 25% allocation instead of 100%, or 3x leverage instead of10x. Either change cuts the loss per stop to about 4–5%.
### I-2 · HIGH — The strategy mix shorts ranges with trend-sized targets
- **The pattern:** in a Range regime with a "Bearish" 1h/4h trend, the scorer picks Range/Grid and the panel agrees on SHORT.- **What happens:** every one of the five trades reached a profit of about +0.8R, then hit the stop. The target sits at +2R (5 ATR).- **What would test it:** a range-specific exit, taking profit near the opposite edge of the range or at about 1R for Range/Grid/MeanReversion only. It should be measured with `scripts/sweep_risk_model.py` or `strategy_backtest` on non-overlapping trades over at least 60 days.- **Do not change it on five trades.** CLAUDE.md records twice that small samples produced findings that reversed at scale.
### I-3 · HIGH — Mid-range entries for range strategies
The Range and Grid shorts entered at RSI 45–57, the middle of the range.
- **What a range short needs:** a location near the top of the range, plus evidence that price is turning down there.- **The proposed fix:** a location check for range strategies only, e.g. "short only in the upper 25% of the recent range".- **Status:** a hypothesis, to be measured before enabling.
### I-4 · HIGH — Every trade is a short
All six recent trades are shorts, on four different coins, while the coins keptbouncing. The 1h/4h "Bearish" label stayed on for days without price falling.Check two things:- that the HTF trend uses closed candles and enough history;- that a Range regime combined with a "Bearish" HTF label is not simply a permanent short bias.
The data is easy to get: `trades.entry_context` already records both values.
### I-5 · MEDIUM — The decisions table cannot explain refusals
All 408 `rejected` rows from the last 3 days come from the switched-off EVENTpath ("entries are originated by the 24-node analysis graph..."). The GRAPHpath's DO_NOT_TRADE verdicts are not written to `decisions`. So`services/abstention` measures the wrong path, and "why didn't it trade?" canonly be answered from the session log.
### I-6 · MEDIUM — Local `.env` has drifted from the server
The local `.env` still names `nemotron-3-super-120b` (404) and `gpt-oss-20b`(times out). The server evidently runs the new models, because its reflectionswork. The local `.env` also points `DATABASE_URL` at the IPv6-only direct host,which cannot be reached from this Mac; the IPv4 pooler works. Keep one sourceof truth.
### I-7 · LOW — Venue minimum at a $3 account
- **The numbers:** equity is $3.21, and the venue minimum order is $5 notional.- **The risk:** at 10x the order still clears the minimum. If you lower the risk as I-1 recommends, small accounts start being refused at the minimum (CLAUDE.md: "a $2 account is BELOW the venue's own floor").- **The safe combination:** about $25 or more in the account, with a lower VaR.
### I-8 · LOW — `RUN_UNTIL_TARGET` switches off the brakes
If the server has `RUN_UNTIL_TARGET=true`, the session floor, the drawdownkillswitch, the daily-loss limit and the re-entry cooldown are all **off**.Only the 50% hard stop remains. Combined with I-1, a losing streak runs intothat hard stop instead of being paused earlier.
